About

Bridging innovation and investment

I work at the point where promising technology meets the discipline of capital.

My focus is long-duration energy storage, particularly vanadium redox flow batteries and other emerging infrastructure technologies that must move beyond technical promise toward credible, repeatable underwriting.

Drawing on economics, investment and project-finance thinking, I help clients identify the gaps between technical performance, contractual protection, insurance, revenue certainty and lender confidence.

Approach

Bankability is a system, not a label

A project becomes financeable when the major sources of uncertainty are connected to evidence, contracts and credible risk owners.

Technical confidence Independent evidence that performance, degradation and operating assumptions are credible.
Contractual confidence Clear obligations, remedies, guarantees and enforceable allocation of responsibility.
Commercial confidence Revenue logic, counterparties and market assumptions that withstand scrutiny.
Financial confidence A risk-return profile that can be understood, priced and repeated.
